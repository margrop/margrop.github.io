Technical Root Cause: Google’s 3-Tier Regional Risk Architecture

From an engineering and regulatory perspective, Google divides its account and authentication infrastructure into a Three-Tier Regional Risk Model. Most developers struggle simply because they confuse the boundaries and authority levels between these three tiers.

图 3：Google 3-Tier Regional Validation Architecture. The network layer is easily spoofed; the payment layer handles billing; but the legal Terms of Service Country is the supreme authority governing advanced AI access.

Layer 1: Network Egress Layer (Network Egress / GeoIP)

  • Mechanism: When a client initiates a TLS handshake, Google’s edge reverse proxy queries MaxMind or internal GeoIP databases to determine the latitude, longitude, ASN, and country code of the client’s public IP.
  • Scope: Web UI localization, CDN edge routing, YouTube regional content catalogs, and Google Search defaults.
  • Authority Level: Weakest. Google’s security engineers know full well that commercial proxies, residential VPNs, and SSH tunnels can alter egress IPs at will. For core export compliance and liability enforcement, Google never relies on IP as an ultimate source of truth.

Layer 2: Payment and Billing Layer (Google Payments Profile)

  • Mechanism: Configured inside payments.google.com, reflecting your billing address, credit card BIN origin, and regional sales tax / VAT identification.
  • Scope: Google Play Store app availability, in-app purchase currency, and subscription pricing (such as paying in USD vs TRY).
  • Authority Level: Moderate. It answers the commercial question: “Who is paying Google, and in what currency?” However, it does not define the overarching legal contract governing your identity.

Layer 3: Legal Terms of Service Country Association (The Core Contract)

  • Mechanism: The foundational legal jurisdiction stamped onto your account at inception based on initial telemetry and registration context.
  • Legal Reality: This property dictates which exact legal entity of Google you have entered into a contract with!
    • In the EEA and Switzerland, you contract with Google Ireland Limited, governed strictly by GDPR and the European AI Act;
    • In the United States, you contract with Google LLC in California, subject to US federal and state jurisdiction;
    • In jurisdictions like Hong Kong or mainland China where developer previews of advanced generative AI models have not been legally cleared, Google lacks the legal safe-harbor protections to offer experimental tools like Antigravity.
  • Authority Level: Absolute Supreme. When Antigravity initiates OAuth authentication, the token service mints a cryptographically signed JWT containing user claims:

"sub": "1082938475928374",
"email": "developer@gmail.com",
"tos_country": "HK",
"tier": "google_one_ai_premium",
"antigravity_eligible": false

} When the Antigravity API gateway inspects this claim and encounters “tos_country”: “HK”, it does not care if your connection IP is in Silicon Valley—it immediately returns HTTP 403 Forbidden!

The Silent Trap: Why Stale Token Caches Keep Antigravity Blocked

After receiving the email confirming your country change to the United States, you open Antigravity, and… it still gives an error! Why?!

This is the most frustrating pitfall in the entire migration journey: the Client-Side Token Cache Deadlock.

图 10：Client-side token cache lifecycle and reset pipeline. Resolving the post-migration block requires an atomic purge of stale OAuth Refresh Tokens to trigger fresh claims negotiation.

Why Token Deadlocks Occur

To avoid bombarding Google’s auth servers on every action, the Antigravity IDE and CLI persist your OAuth Refresh Token, Access Token, and initial onboarding claims locally on disk:

  • On Windows: %USERPROFILE%.gemini\antigravity-cli</code>
  • On Linux / macOS: ~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/

When your account was still bound to the old region, Antigravity fetched a token stamped with country: HK, eligible: false and saved it locally.

Even after Google updates your account in the cloud, your local client will keep reusing this stale token until it naturally expires (often weeks later)! As long as the client sends the stale token, the API gateway will faithfully respond with 403 Forbidden!

The 2-Step Deadlock Breaker:

  1. In the Antigravity IDE / VS Code:
    • Press Cmd+Shift+P (macOS) or Ctrl+Shift+P (Windows/Linux) to open the Command Palette;
    • Type and execute: Antigravity: Reset Onboarding;
    • Click Sign In and complete the clean OAuth flow in your browser.
  2. In the Terminal (CLI):
    • Run our automated script below, or move the stale auth*.json and state*.json files out of ~/.gemini/antigravity-cli/.
    • Run agy models again. The CLI will request fresh authentication. The newly minted JWT token will carry your verified US claims, instantly granting access to full Pro models!

Native PowerShell 5.1/7+ script designed for Windows 11 and Windows Terminal:

<#
.SYNOPSIS
    Google Account Terms of Service Country Association & Antigravity Diagnostic
    Target Platform: Windows 11 (PowerShell 5.1 / 7+)
    Zero Third-Party Dependencies: Native PowerShell cmdlets & browser handlers
    Dual-Mode: Interactive Human Mode & Non-Interactive AI Agent Mode (-Agent / -Json)
#>

[CmdletBinding()]
param (
    [Alias("Json")]
    [switch]$Agent
)

$ErrorActionPreference = "SilentlyContinue"
[Console]::OutputEncoding = [System.Text.Encoding]::UTF8

$TosFormUrl = "https://policies.google.com/country-association-form"
$TosTermsUrl = "https://policies.google.com/terms"
$PaymentsUrl = "https://pay.google.com"

# 1. Detect Egress Country Code (Preserves IP privacy)
function Get-EgressCountry {
    $cc = ""
    try {
        $trace = Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "https://cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace" -TimeoutSec 4
        if ($trace -match "loc=([A-Z]{2})") {
            $cc = $matches[1]
        }
    } catch {}

    if ([string]::IsNullOrWhiteSpace($cc)) {
        try {
            $cc = (Invoke-RestMethod -Uri "https://ipapi.co/country/" -TimeoutSec 4).Trim()
        } catch {}
    }

    if ([string]::IsNullOrWhiteSpace($cc)) { $cc = "UNKNOWN" }
    return $cc
}

# 2. Inspect Antigravity CLI and Cache Paths
function Get-AntigravityPaths {
    $cliPath = ""
    $cmd = Get-Command "agy.exe" -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
    if ($cmd) {
        $cliPath = $cmd.Source
    } else {
        $defaultAgy = Join-Path $env:USERPROFILE ".local\bin\agy.exe"
        if (Test-Path $defaultAgy) { $cliPath = $defaultAgy }
    }

    $cacheDir = Join-Path $env:USERPROFILE ".gemini\antigravity-cli"
    $ideCache = Join-Path $env:LOCALAPPDATA "Google\Antigravity"

    return [PSCustomObject]@{
        CliPath  = $cliPath
        CacheDir = $cacheDir
        IdeCache = $ideCache
    }
}

# 3. Safely Backup and Purge Stale Tokens
function Purge-StaleCache ([string]$CacheDir) {
    $timestamp = Get-Date -Format "yyyyMMddHHmmss"
    $backupDir = "${CacheDir}_backup_${timestamp}"
    $filesBackedUp = 0

    if (Test-Path $CacheDir) {
        New-Item -ItemType Directory -Path $backupDir -Force | Out-Null
        $targets = Get-ChildItem -Path $CacheDir -Include "auth*.json", "state*.json", "*token*", "session*" -Recurse -File -ErrorAction SilentlyContinue
        foreach ($file in $targets) {
            Move-Item -Path $file.FullName -Destination $backupDir -Force
            $filesBackedUp++
        }
    }

    return [PSCustomObject]@{
        BackupDir     = $backupDir
        FilesBackedUp = $filesBackedUp
    }
}

$egressCountry = Get-EgressCountry
$paths = Get-AntigravityPaths

# AI Agent Mode: Clean JSON Output
if ($Agent) {
    $purgeResult = Purge-StaleCache -CacheDir $paths.CacheDir
    $result = [PSCustomObject]@{
        status            = "success"
        platform          = "Windows 11"
        egress_country    = $egressCountry
        antigravity_cli   = $paths.CliPath
        cache_directory   = $paths.CacheDir
        backup_directory  = $purgeResult.BackupDir
        files_purged      = $purgeResult.FilesBackedUp
        official_form_url = $TosFormUrl
        terms_url         = $TosTermsUrl
        payments_url      = $PaymentsUrl
        action_required   = "Submit country update to United States (US) at official_form_url, then re-authenticate agy or trigger Antigravity: Reset Onboarding in IDE."
    }
    $result | ConvertTo-Json -Compress
    exit 0
}

# Interactive Human Mode
Clear-Host
Write-Host "================================================================================" -ForegroundColor Cyan
Write-Host " Google One Pro x Antigravity Region Diagnostic & Auto-Fix Tool (Windows 11)" -ForegroundColor White
Write-Host "================================================================================" -ForegroundColor Cyan
Write-Host ""

Write-Host "[*] Step 1: Network Egress Detection..." -ForegroundColor Yellow
Write-Host "    Detected Egress Region Code: $egressCountry" -ForegroundColor White
if ($egressCountry -ne "US") {
    Write-Host "    [!] Notice: Egress region is '$egressCountry'. We recommend routing through a US node" -ForegroundColor Red
    Write-Host "        prior to submitting the Google Country Association Form for instant approval." -ForegroundColor Red
} else {
    Write-Host "    [+] Egress region is US. Ideal for immediate review approval." -ForegroundColor Green
}
Write-Host ""

Write-Host "[*] Step 2: Checking Antigravity Environment..." -ForegroundColor Yellow
if ($paths.CliPath) {
    Write-Host "    [+] Detected Antigravity CLI at: $($paths.CliPath)" -ForegroundColor Green
} else {
    Write-Host "    [-] CLI not found in PATH (Normal if using IDE extension only)." -ForegroundColor DarkGray
}
Write-Host "    Cache Directory: $($paths.CacheDir)" -ForegroundColor White
Write-Host ""

Write-Host "[*] Step 3: Launching Official Review Portal in Default Browser..." -ForegroundColor Yellow
Write-Host "    URL: $TosFormUrl" -ForegroundColor White
Start-Process $TosFormUrl
Write-Host "    [+] Browser launched. Verify your current country and submit 'United States'." -ForegroundColor Green
Write-Host ""

$confirm = Read-Host "[?] Safely backup and purge local stale OAuth token cache to prepare for clean re-auth? (y/N)"
if ($confirm -match "^[Yy]$") {
    $purgeResult = Purge-StaleCache -CacheDir $paths.CacheDir
    Write-Host "    [+] Outdated tokens backed up to: $($purgeResult.BackupDir)" -ForegroundColor Green
    Write-Host "    [+] Purged $($purgeResult.FilesBackedUp) stale session file(s)." -ForegroundColor Green
    Write-Host ""
    Write-Host "================================================================================" -ForegroundColor Cyan
    Write-Host " Next Steps to Activate Antigravity Pro Quota:" -ForegroundColor Yellow
    Write-Host " 1. Wait 5-15 mins for the Google Country Association confirmation email." -ForegroundColor White
    Write-Host " 2. In Antigravity IDE / VS Code: press Ctrl+Shift+P -> 'Antigravity: Reset Onboarding'." -ForegroundColor White

}

POSIX-compliant Bash script handling both headless cloud servers and desktop GUI installations:

#!/usr/bin/env bash
# ==============================================================================
# Google Account Terms of Service Country Association & Antigravity Diagnostic
# Target Platform: Ubuntu 26.04 LTS (and modern Linux distributions)
# Zero Third-Party Dependencies: Uses native curl, awk, bash, and xdg-open
# Dual-Mode: Interactive Human Mode & Non-Interactive AI Agent Mode (--agent / --json)
# ==============================================================================

set -euo pipefail

MODE="interactive"
if [[ "${1:-}" == "--agent" || "${1:-}" == "--json" ]]; then
  MODE="agent"
fi

TOS_FORM_URL="https://policies.google.com/country-association-form"
TOS_TERMS_URL="https://policies.google.com/terms"
PAYMENTS_URL="https://pay.google.com"

detect_egress_country() {
  local cc
  cc=$(curl -s --connect-timeout 4 https://cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace 2>/dev/null | awk -F= '$1=="loc"{print $2}')
  if [[ -z "$cc" ]]; then
    cc=$(curl -s --connect-timeout 4 https://ipapi.co/country/ 2>/dev/null || true)
  fi
  if [[ -z "$cc" ]]; then
    cc="UNKNOWN"
  fi
  echo "$cc"
}

check_antigravity_paths() {
  local cli_path=""
  if command -v agy >/dev/null 2>&1; then
    cli_path=$(command -v agy)
  elif [[ -x "$HOME/.local/bin/agy" ]]; then
    cli_path="$HOME/.local/bin/agy"
  elif [[ -x "/usr/local/bin/agy" ]]; then
    cli_path="/usr/local/bin/agy"
  fi

  local cache_dir="$HOME/.gemini/antigravity-cli"
  local ide_cache="$HOME/.config/Google/Antigravity"
  
  echo "$cli_path|$cache_dir|$ide_cache"
}

purge_stale_cache() {
  local cache_dir="$1"
  local backup_dir="${cache_dir}_backup_$(date +%Y%m%d%H%M%S)"
  local files_backed_up=0

  if [[ -d "$cache_dir" ]]; then
    mkdir -p "$backup_dir"
    for f in "$cache_dir"/auth*.json "$cache_dir"/state*.json "$cache_dir"/*token* "$cache_dir"/session*; do
      if [[ -e "$f" ]]; then
        mv "$f" "$backup_dir/"
        ((files_backed_up++)) || true
      fi
    done
  fi

  echo "$backup_dir|$files_backed_up"
}

open_browser() {
  local url="$1"
  if [[ -n "${DISPLAY:-}" || -n "${WAYLAND_DISPLAY:-}" ]]; then
    if command -v xdg-open >/dev/null 2>&1; then
      xdg-open "$url" >/dev/null 2>&1 &
      return 0
    fi
  fi
  return 1
}

EGRESS_COUNTRY=$(detect_egress_country)
IFS='|' read -r CLI_PATH CACHE_DIR IDE_CACHE < <(check_antigravity_paths)

if [[ "$MODE" == "agent" ]]; then
  IFS='|' read -r BACKUP_DIR FILES_BACKED < <(purge_stale_cache "$CACHE_DIR")
  cat <<EOF
{
  "status": "success",
  "platform": "Ubuntu 26.04",
  "egress_country": "${EGRESS_COUNTRY}",
  "antigravity_cli": "${CLI_PATH}",
  "cache_directory": "${CACHE_DIR}",
  "backup_directory": "${BACKUP_DIR}",
  "files_purged": ${FILES_BACKED},
  "official_form_url": "${TOS_FORM_URL}",
  "terms_url": "${TOS_TERMS_URL}",
  "payments_url": "${PAYMENTS_URL}",
  "action_required": "Submit country update to United States (US) at official_form_url, then re-authenticate agy or trigger Antigravity: Reset Onboarding in IDE."
}
EOF
  exit 0
fi

clear || true
echo "================================================================================"
echo " Google One Pro × Antigravity Region Diagnostic & Auto-Fix Tool (Ubuntu)"
echo "================================================================================"
echo ""
echo "[*] Step 1: Network Egress Detection..."
echo "    Detected Egress Region Code: ${EGRESS_COUNTRY}"
if [[ "$EGRESS_COUNTRY" != "US" ]]; then
  echo "    [!] Notice: Egress is '${EGRESS_COUNTRY}'. Route through a US proxy for rapid approval."
else
  echo "    [✓] Egress region is US. Ideal setup."
fi
echo ""

echo "[*] Step 2: Checking Antigravity Environment..."
if [[ -n "$CLI_PATH" ]]; then
  echo "    [✓] Detected Antigravity CLI at: ${CLI_PATH}"
else
  echo "    [-] CLI not found in PATH (Normal if using IDE extension only)."
fi
echo "    Cache Directory: ${CACHE_DIR}"
echo ""

echo "[*] Step 3: Google Country Association Review Link..."
echo "    URL: ${TOS_FORM_URL}"
if open_browser "${TOS_FORM_URL}"; then
  echo "    [✓] Graphical browser launched."
else
  echo "    [-] Headless/SSH environment detected. Please open the link above in your host browser."
fi
echo ""

read -r -p "[?] Safely backup and purge local stale OAuth token cache? (y/N): " CONFIRM
if [[ "$CONFIRM" =~ ^[Yy]$ ]]; then
  IFS='|' read -r BACKUP_DIR FILES_BACKED < <(purge_stale_cache "$CACHE_DIR")
  echo "    [✓] Outdated tokens backed up to: ${BACKUP_DIR}"
  echo "    [✓] Cleaned ${FILES_BACKED} stale session file(s)."
  echo ""
  echo "================================================================================"

3. macOS 27: google-tos-antigravity-fix-macos.sh

Tailored for Apple Silicon and Intel macOS, integrating natively with open:

#!/usr/bin/env bash
# ==============================================================================
# Google Account Terms of Service Country Association & Antigravity Diagnostic
# Target Platform: macOS 27 (Apple Silicon & Intel)
# Zero Third-Party Dependencies: Uses native curl, awk, zsh/bash, and open
# Dual-Mode: Interactive Human Mode & Non-Interactive AI Agent Mode (--agent / --json)
# ==============================================================================

set -euo pipefail

MODE="interactive"
if [[ "${1:-}" == "--agent" || "${1:-}" == "--json" ]]; then
  MODE="agent"
fi

TOS_FORM_URL="https://policies.google.com/country-association-form"
TOS_TERMS_URL="https://policies.google.com/terms"
PAYMENTS_URL="https://pay.google.com"

detect_egress_country() {
  local cc
  cc=$(curl -s --connect-timeout 4 https://cloudflare.com/cdn-cgi/trace 2>/dev/null | awk -F= '$1=="loc"{print $2}')
  if [[ -z "$cc" ]]; then
    cc=$(curl -s --connect-timeout 4 https://ipapi.co/country/ 2>/dev/null || true)
  fi
  if [[ -z "$cc" ]]; then
    cc="UNKNOWN"
  fi
  echo "$cc"
}

check_antigravity_paths() {
  local cli_path=""
  if command -v agy >/dev/null 2>&1; then
    cli_path=$(command -v agy)
  elif [[ -x "$HOME/.local/bin/agy" ]]; then
    cli_path="$HOME/.local/bin/agy"
  fi

  local cache_dir="$HOME/.gemini/antigravity-cli"
  local ide_cache="$HOME/Library/Application Support/Google/Antigravity"
  
  echo "$cli_path|$cache_dir|$ide_cache"
}

purge_stale_cache() {
  local cache_dir="$1"
  local backup_dir="${cache_dir}_backup_$(date +%Y%m%d%H%M%S)"
  local files_backed_up=0

  if [[ -d "$cache_dir" ]]; then
    mkdir -p "$backup_dir"
    for f in "$cache_dir"/auth*.json "$cache_dir"/state*.json "$cache_dir"/*token* "$cache_dir"/session*; do
      if [[ -e "$f" ]]; then
        mv "$f" "$backup_dir/"
        ((files_backed_up++)) || true
      fi
    done
  fi

  echo "$backup_dir|$files_backed_up"
}

EGRESS_COUNTRY=$(detect_egress_country)
IFS='|' read -r CLI_PATH CACHE_DIR IDE_CACHE < <(check_antigravity_paths)

if [[ "$MODE" == "agent" ]]; then
  IFS='|' read -r BACKUP_DIR FILES_BACKED < <(purge_stale_cache "$CACHE_DIR")
  cat <<EOF
{
  "status": "success",
  "platform": "macOS 27",
  "egress_country": "${EGRESS_COUNTRY}",
  "antigravity_cli": "${CLI_PATH}",
  "cache_directory": "${CACHE_DIR}",
  "backup_directory": "${BACKUP_DIR}",
  "files_purged": ${FILES_BACKED},
  "official_form_url": "${TOS_FORM_URL}",
  "terms_url": "${TOS_TERMS_URL}",
  "payments_url": "${PAYMENTS_URL}",
  "action_required": "Please submit country update to United States (US) via official_form_url, then re-authenticate agy or run Antigravity: Reset Onboarding in IDE."
}
EOF
  exit 0
fi

clear || true
echo "================================================================================"
echo " Google One Pro × Antigravity Region Diagnostic & Auto-Fix Tool (macOS)"
echo "================================================================================"
echo ""
echo "[*] Step 1: Network Egress Detection..."
echo "    Detected Egress Region Code: ${EGRESS_COUNTRY}"
if [[ "$EGRESS_COUNTRY" != "US" ]]; then
  echo "    [!] Notice: Egress is '${EGRESS_COUNTRY}'. A US proxy is recommended for fast approval."
else
  echo "    [✓] Egress region is US. Ideal for review."
fi
echo ""

echo "[*] Step 2: Checking Local Antigravity Environment..."
if [[ -n "$CLI_PATH" ]]; then
  echo "    [✓] Antigravity CLI detected at: ${CLI_PATH}"
else
  echo "    [-] Antigravity CLI not found in PATH (Normal if using IDE extension only)."
fi
echo "    Cache Directory: ${CACHE_DIR}"
echo ""

echo "[*] Step 3: Launching Official Review Portal in Default Browser..."
echo "    URL: ${TOS_FORM_URL}"
open "${TOS_FORM_URL}" 2>/dev/null || true
echo "    [✓] Browser launched. Please select 'United States' and submit."
echo ""

read -r -p "[?] Safely backup and purge local stale OAuth token cache? (y/N): " CONFIRM
if [[ "$CONFIRM" =~ ^[Yy]$ ]]; then
  IFS='|' read -r BACKUP_DIR FILES_BACKED < <(purge_stale_cache "$CACHE_DIR")
  echo "    [✓] Outdated tokens backed up to: ${BACKUP_DIR}"
  echo "    [✓] Purged ${FILES_BACKED} stale session file(s)."
  echo ""
  echo "================================================================================"
  echo " Next Steps to Activate Antigravity Pro Quota:"
  echo " 1. Wait 5-15 mins for the Google Country Association confirmation email."
  echo " 2. In Antigravity IDE / VS Code: press Cmd+Shift+P -> 'Antigravity: Reset Onboarding'."
  echo " 3. Sign in again via your Google account."
  echo " 4. In Terminal: run 'agy models' to enjoy unrestricted Pro capacity!"
  echo "================================================================================"

Frequently Asked Questions and Best Practices

Q1: Will updating my Terms of Service Country to the United States delete my Google Drive files, Gmail history, or cancel my YouTube Premium?

Answer: Absolutely not. Updating your Terms of Service Country Association merely changes the legal contracting affiliate of Google that governs your relationship (e.g. from Google Ireland or Google Asia Pacific to Google LLC). All your cloud storage, emails, photos, and files remain completely untouched. Existing subscriptions such as YouTube Premium will continue uninterrupted as long as your underlying Google Pay payment method remains active.

Q2: What if the submission form returns an error stating “Unable to process request”?

Answer: This is typically caused by two factors:

  1. Data Center IP Flagging: The IP address you are routing through is categorized as an abused commercial hosting data center. Fix: Switch to a clean residential or private proxy and re-open the form in an incognito window.
  2. Google Family Group Lock: If your account is a subordinate member of a Google Family Group, your account’s country association is pinned to the Family Manager’s jurisdiction. Fix: You must temporarily leave the family group, submit the country change independently, and rejoin afterward.

Q3: Can I change my country association again in the future if I travel?

Answer: Yes, but avoid frequent jumping! While the Terms of Service review form does not impose the strict 365-day lockout found in the Google Play Store, rapid back-and-forth switching across multiple jurisdictions within short intervals will flag your account for manual anti-abuse review. We strongly advise selecting the United States and keeping it permanent, as the US market consistently receives first-tier access to all flagship Google AI features.
